# GLM Design Contract (retired)

Retired on 6 November 2026 (UX-115). Its successor is `GLM.Web/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` in the Foundant/glm repo, which states what production ships today, and the `/Dev/DesignSystem` page in GLM.Web, which renders it.

Engineering owns shipped components and their documentation there. Design owns design system decisions, which are tracked in that document's Open decisions table and on the UX Jira board. Proposals that production has not shipped live in this repo, in `glm-prototype-foundation.css`, and are shown in the kitchen sink at https://glm-design-system.netlify.app/.

The last full version of this contract (v1.1, with its backlog items 1 to 37 and correction history) is in git history at commit `4df3746`. Read it there for how a past decision was reached, not for current rules.
